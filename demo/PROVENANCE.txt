This is an unmodified copy of SCORCH_NA_processed_1996_2025.nc from:
ERA5 Hourly and Processed Daily Maximum Temperature for the scoRch.simulator
North America Demonstration, April–September 1996–2025, version 1.0.0.
Creators: Fawaz Bouhamad and Nasser Najibi, University of Florida.
Dataset DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24232552

Coverage: 25–50 N and 130–58 W, April–September 1996–2025;
1,800 one-degree cells, 5,490 dates and 9,882,000 Tmax values in Celsius.
The authors calculated the maximum of 24 hourly 2-m temperatures per UTC
day at each native 0.25-degree point, subtracted 273.15, then averaged each
complete 4x4 block into a one-degree cell. Each point has equal weight 1/16.
No latitude/area or temperature weighting is used for this aggregation.
Points belong to [k,k+1) degree cells with centres k+0.5; the incomplete
upper-edge cells beginning at 50 N and -58 E are excluded.
Processing and output use float64, with no decimal rounding or integer packing.
