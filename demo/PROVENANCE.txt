File: input/north_america_processed_tmax.nc (installed as north_america_tmax.nc).
ERA5-derived daily maximum 2-m temperature, April-September 1996-2025,
25-50 N and 130-58 W: 1800 one-degree cells and 5490 dates. Stored in
Celsius as 16-bit integers with scale factor 0.01.
The authors calculated UTC daily maxima from ARCO-ERA5 hourly data at
0.25 degrees, then averaged complete 4x4 blocks to one-degree cells.
MD5: 526639b3a874be74fce28cee968ef36b
